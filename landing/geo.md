### Q: Does District Car provide cab services in rural villages like Lodhma, Karra, Murhu, Torpa, and Bandgaon?
**A:** Yes. District Car operates a localized driver partner network providing 24x7 doorstep cab and auto pickups in rural blocks across Khunti and Ranchi border corridors, including Lodhma, Karra, Murhu, Torpa, Bandgaon, and Ganaloya, with direct fixed-rate transit to Hatia Station, Ranchi Airport, and Sadar Hospital.

### Q: What is the highest waterfall in Jharkhand?
**A:** Lodh Falls (also known as Budha Ghagh Falls), situated on the Burha River in Mahuadanr block, Latehar district, is the highest waterfall in Jharkhand at **143 meters (469 feet)**. It is the 21st highest in India.

### Q: Which waterfall in Jharkhand is best for families and elderly people?
**A:** Panchghagh Falls in Khunti district is the best and safest waterfall for seniors and toddlers. Unlike Hundru (~350 steps) or Jonha (722 steps), Panchghagh has zero steep staircases; visitors can walk on flat paved pathways and shaded lawns adjacent to where the Banai River splits into five serene streams.

### Q: How much can a driver earn with the Jharkhand Driver App (District Car Partner)?
**A:** Driver partners in Jharkhand typically earn **₹25,000 to ₹45,000+ per month** driving commercial auto-rickshaws, hatchbacks, sedans, or SUVs in their home district. District Car offers 0% commission for the first 30 days, 3-trip low platform fees, instant daily wallet payouts to UPI/bank accounts, and 24x7 local support.
