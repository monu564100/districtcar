Yes! District Car has a dedicated category for 3-wheelers and E-Rickshaws for town-to-village commutes, railway station pick-ups, and daily sabzi mandi runs.

Do I need a commercial yellow plate?

Commercial yellow plates are preferred for outstation tourism. For local district trips, vehicles compliant with Jharkhand state transport commercial rules are welcomed.
