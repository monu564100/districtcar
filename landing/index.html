Why District Car?

Built Specially for Lower Cities & Gramin Jharkhand

Urban ride apps cater strictly to tier-1 metro centers. When commuters in Khunti, Simdega, Gumla, or Giridih need a cab, they face empty return extortion. District Car solves this with rural-first mobility.

Medical Emergency & Sadar Hospital Lifeline

Direct doorstep pickup from remote tolas to Sadar Hospitals, RIMS Ranchi, and medical centers. Zero cancellation during emergencies.

Round Trip Waiting & Guaranteed Return

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking rides, fares, and partnering in Jharkhand.

Urban cab aggregators work almost exclusively within Ranchi and Jamshedpur municipal limits and rarely accept rides into rural blocks or villages. Even if they accept, they demand double cash for empty return trips. District Car is engineered for rural and district inter-connectivity with guaranteed round-trip return pricing and doorstep village pickup.
When you book a Round-Trip (e.g. from a village in Khunti to Sadar Hospital Ranchi and back), our system gives a 35% discount on the return leg and includes your waiting time (up to specified hours). This saves passengers between ₹200 to ₹800 compared to booking two one-way rides.
You can directly call or WhatsApp our official Jharkhand desk at +91 87896 01387 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
Cash payments collected from passengers belong to the driver immediately. For online UPI/card payments, earnings are deposited directly into the driver’s registered bank account on a daily settlement cycle without waiting for weeks.
Both the District Car Customer App and Driver Partner (सारथी) App are officially published and live on the Google Play Store! You can install them safely with direct links on this website or search "District Car" on Google Play.
