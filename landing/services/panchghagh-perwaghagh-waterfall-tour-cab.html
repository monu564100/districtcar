Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is included in the Twin Falls waterfall tour package?

The package includes doorstep pickup from your location in Ranchi or Khunti, comfortable AC car with fuel, all toll and parking charges, and 4 to 5 hours of waiting time while you picnic and sightsee.

Can we combine Panchghagh Falls with Baba Amreshwar Dham temple?

Yes! Baba Amreshwar Dham (Angrabari) is on the way, and our drivers will happily stop for darshan as part of the full-day tour.

Are these waterfalls safe for families and children?

Yes, both Panchghagh and Perwaghagh have government watchtowers, safety railings, local forest guards, and clean sitting spots.

How do I book a waterfall picnic cab?
