## Complete 24 District Geographic & Transportation Index

### 1. Ranchi District (Capital Hub)
- **Headquarters:** Ranchi (23.3441° N, 85.3096° E) | Elevation: 651 m.
- **Key Blocks:** Kanke, Ormanjhi, Angara, Namkum, Hatia, Bundu, Ratu, Itki, Bero.
- **Top Attractions:** Hundru Falls (98m), Dassam Falls (44m), Jonha Falls (43m), Sita Falls, Surya Mandir Bundu, Jagannath Temple, Rock Garden, Patratu Valley link.
- **Major Transport Hubs:** Birsa Munda Airport (IXR), Ranchi Junction (RNC), Hatia Station (HTE).
- **District Car Service:** 24x7 doorstep city cabs, airport transfers, rural village auto/cabs, outstation sightseeing packages from ₹899.

### 2. Khunti District (Tribal Heartland & Lac Capital)
- **Headquarters:** Khunti (23.0722° N, 85.2789° E) | Elevation: 611 m.
- **Key Blocks:** Murhu, Torpa, Karra, Rania, Arki, Khunti.
