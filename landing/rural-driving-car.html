Frequently Asked Questions (Rural Driving Car)

How will the driver find my village if there is no exact street address?

District Car integrates local landmark pinpoints (such as school, panchayat bhawan, high school ground, temple, or chowk). Furthermore, our local drivers are from your neighborhood and know the rural lanes by name.

Can I book a ride by calling on the phone instead of using the app?

Yes! If you have low data or an elder is booking without a smartphone, simply dial our 24x7 rural dispatch desk at +91 87896 01387.
