Murhu, Torpa & Bandgaon Travel Guide 2026: Scenic Ghats, Lac Forests & Heritage Routes

District Car Travel Desk October 2026 8 min read Scenic Ghats

⚡ Circuit Overview: The Southern Forest Corridor

Geography: Southern Khunti extending into the northern hills of West Singhbhum.

Highlights: Murhu Lac trading hub, Torpa Perwaghagh falls, Bandgaon mountain ghat passes, Ganaloya forest hamlets.

Driving Terrain: Smooth highway giving way to thrilling mountain curves, hairpin bends, and virgin Sal valleys.

Recommended Vehicle: High-ground clearance Sedans and SUVs available on District Car.

Beyond the bustling district towns of Jharkhand lies a region of raw, untamed majesty. The southern axis connecting Murhu, Torpa, and Bandgaon—stretching into the forested hamlets of Ganaloya and the Porahat mountain range—is one of eastern India’s most scenic and least explored road trip corridors.

Here, emerald Sal tree forests meet ancient granite escarpments, and winding mountain passes drop gracefully from the 2,000-foot Ranchi-Khunti plateau into the lush river basins of West Singhbhum.

Key Stops Along the Murhu-Torpa-Bandgaon Axis

1. Murhu — The Lac Capital & Gateway to Panchghagh

Located 10 km south of Khunti along the Chaibasa road, Murhu is the commercial epicentre of India’s natural shellac trade. On weekly market days, tribal cultivators arrive with bags of raw sticklac scraped from forest trees. Murhu also serves as the direct gateway to Panchghagh Falls and the surrounding eco-tourism parks.

2. Torpa & The Emerald Waters of Perwaghagh

Branching west from Murhu brings you to Torpa. Hidden inside the deep forest ravines near Fatka village lies Perwaghagh Falls. Named after pigeons (Perwa in Mundari) that nest inside rock crevices behind the waterfall, this secluded cascade pours into a strikingly deep, turquoise-green natural canyon pool that looks straight out of an international travel documentary.

3. Bandgaon Ghats — The Mountain Pass into West Singhbhum

Continuing south past Murhu towards Chakradharpur, the landscape transforms dramatically as you enter the Bandgaon Ghats. The 2-lane highway navigates a thrilling sequence of hairpin bends carving through the rugged Porahat mountain range. Dense bamboo thickets, mist-shrouded valley viewpoints, and babbling jungle rivulets line both sides of the roadway.

4. Ganaloya & Porahat Tribal Forest Hamlets

The remote rural hamlets surrounding Ganaloya and the Porahat forest reserve preserve age-old traditions of organic farming, mud-walled houses decorated with white clay rice paste art (Khabar/Sohrai motifs), and tranquil sacred groves (Jaherthan) where ancient forest deities are venerated.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are there good dhabas to eat along the Bandgaon road?

Yes, Murhu and Torpa town centers have clean family dhabas serving hot roti, dal tadka, desi chicken, and fresh local sweets.

Can small hatchbacks drive on Bandgaon ghats?

Yes, the main state highway is fully paved and tarmac-surfaced. However, for interior detours towards Perwaghagh or forest hamlets, an SUV is strongly recommended.
