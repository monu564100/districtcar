Known for: Temperate climate, colonial heritage bungalows, and UNESCO-recognized Sohrai & Khovar tribal mural art.

Perched at a cool elevation of 2,000 feet on the northern edge of the Chotanagpur plateau, Hazaribagh—literally the "City of a Thousand Gardens"—has historically served as a popular sanatorium and wellness retreat. Its mild, pleasant year-round climate, extensive pine and eucalyptus forests, and tranquil artificial lakes make it an effortless weekend getaway from Ranchi, Bokaro, and Dhanbad.

Top Places to Visit in Hazaribagh

1. Canary Hill — Iconic Sunset Watchtower

Rising on the northern outskirts of the town, Canary Hill is covered in thick sal and eucalyptus groves. At the summit sits an iconic multi-storey rest house and watchtower. Climbing to the top rewards travelers with panoramic sunset views over the entire Hazaribagh township, rolling hillocks, and distant forest lines.

2. Hazaribagh Wildlife Sanctuary

Covering 184 square kilometers of pristine tropical dry deciduous jungle, this sanctuary is home to sambar, nilgai, wild boars, spotted deer, and diverse avian fauna. Well-laid masonry watchtowers near natural salt licks provide visitors with excellent wildlife spotting opportunities.

3. Konar Dam

Constructed as part of the Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC) on the Konar River, this massive masonry dam features beautiful shoreline gardens, boating facilities, and scenic sunset views across its sprawling reservoir.

4. Sohrai & Khovar Tribal Village Art

Hazaribagh is the global center of the GI-tagged Sohrai and Khovar wall painting traditions. Practiced predominantly by tribal women in villages like Bhelwara and Isco, the murals are painted using natural earth pigments and chewed twigs to depict forest wildlife, peacocks, and fertility symbols.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How are the road conditions between Ranchi and Hazaribagh?

The road is an ultra-smooth, 4-lane national expressway (NH-20) with modern toll plazas, excellent bypasses, and pleasant food plazas at Ramgarh.

Can I visit Canary Hill in the evening?

Yes. The park around Canary Hill is open until 6:00 PM, making it the premier location in Hazaribagh to catch the sunset.
