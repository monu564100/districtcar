Dassam Falls (Da-song) Ranchi: Timings, Viewpoints, Safety & Cab Booking Guide 2026

District Car Travel Desk October 2026 8 min read Ranchi Waterfalls

⚡ Quick Facts: Dassam Falls

Height: 44 meters (144 feet) vertical drop.

River: Kanchi River, plunging over steep vertical cliff ledges.

Distance from Ranchi: 40 km via NH-33 (Tata Highway) near Taimara village.

Amenities: Multi-tier viewing decks, tourist canteen, paved parking, safety barriers.

Round-Trip Cab Fare: Starting at ₹1,299 via District Car.

Located just 40 kilometers southeast of Ranchi on the newly expanded 4-lane Tata-Ranchi Expressway (NH-33), Dassam Falls is celebrated for its sheer raw force and theatrical geological drama. In the local Mundari tongue, the cascade is revered as Da-song, meaning "water pouring forth in song."

As the Kanchi River arrives at the edge of the plateau near Taimara village, it fractures into ten foaming white streams plunging 44 meters straight down into a deep, misty emerald gorge surrounded by towering sandstone crags.

How to Reach Dassam Falls from Ranchi & Jamshedpur

  • From Ranchi (40 km, ~55 mins): Drive south along NH-33 towards Jamshedpur. Cross Rampur and Namkum, continue past Taimara toll plaza, and take the well-marked right turn onto Dassam Falls approach road (4 km well-paved road to entrance gate).
  • From Jamshedpur (90 km, ~1 hr 45 mins): Head north on NH-33 past Chandil and Bundu towards Ranchi, turning left at Taimara junction.

Combine with the Surya Mandir (Sun Temple) Bundu

Just 14 km further down the highway from the Dassam turnoff lies the striking Surya Mandir Bundu. Built in the shape of a colossal celestial chariot with 18 elaborately carved wheels pulled by 7 galloping stone horses, it is one of the most photographed temples in eastern India. A sacred freshwater pond inside the campus adds to its serene ambiance.

Safety Precautions at Dassam Falls

While modernized tourist platforms provide breathtaking viewpoints, visitor safety is paramount:

  • Strictly No Swimming: The plunge pool of Dassam has deep natural crevices and powerful underwater suction currents. Swimming is prohibited by district administration.
  • Remain on Observation Platforms: Do not climb over the steel railings to stand on slippery wet rocks for selfies.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What are the opening hours of Dassam Falls?

The gates are open from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM daily. Early morning hours between 9:00 AM and 11:30 AM offer the clearest light and smallest crowds.

Is the staircase descent difficult?

The modernized concrete steps (~250 steps) have sturdy railings and rest landings, making the walk down and back up manageable for all average-fitness travelers.
