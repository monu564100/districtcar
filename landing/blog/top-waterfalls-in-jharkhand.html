In-Depth Review of Top 5 Cascades

1. Lodh Falls (Latehar) — The 143-Meter Untamed Giant

Lodh Falls in Mahuadanr valley is Jharkhand's tallest waterfall and ranks 21st across India. The Burha river drops thunderously through dense sal and mahua groves, creating a roar that can be heard miles away. Because it is 215 km from Ranchi, many nature lovers combine it with a trip to Netarhat hill station.

2. Hundru Falls — The Crown Jewel of Ranchi

Cascading from 98 meters over polished granite rocks on the Subarnarekha River, Hundru is a photographer's paradise. The base features natural bathing pools and viewing decks. There are around 350 stone steps equipped with sturdy handrails and rest pavilions.

3. Dassam Falls — 10 Roaring Torrents

Derived from the Mundari word Da-song, Dassam Falls splits the Kanchi River into 10 foaming channels plunging over jagged vertical cliffs. Glass skywalk viewpoints and modernized safety railings make it one of the most visited cascades near Ranchi.

4. Jonha Falls — Gautamdhara's Sacred Cascade

Surrounded by tranquil forests, Jonha Falls is celebrated both for its natural beauty and its Buddhist heritage. An ancient Lord Buddha shrine sits near the steps. During Kartik Purnima and Makar Sankranti, tribal cultural fairs gather thousands of pilgrims.

5. Panchghagh Falls — The Ultimate Family Picnic Paradise

Unlike Hundru or Jonha which require hundreds of stairs, Panchghagh in Khunti district is situated on flat picnic terrain. The Banai River splits into 5 gentle streams over wide granite ledges, making it the safest waterfall for senior citizens and toddlers.
