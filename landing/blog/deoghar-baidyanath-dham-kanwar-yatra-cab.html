Deoghar Baidyanath Dham Yatra 2026: VIP Darshan Pass & Outstation Cab Guide

District Car Travel Desk October 2026 8 min read Pilgrimage & Temple Tour

⚡ Essential Yatra Facts

Significance: One of the 12 sacred Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva and a Shaktipeeth.

Distance from Ranchi: 250 km via Giridih / Jamtara road (approx. 5.5 hours drive).

VIP Darshan Pass (शीघ्र दर्शनम): ₹500 per devotee at temple admin counter.

Cab Booking: Sanitized AC Sedans and SUVs available on District Car app.

Nestled in the Santhal Parganas division, Baidyanath Dham in Deoghar is one of Hinduism's most sacred pilgrimage destinations. Mythologically, it is where Ravana attempted to carry Lord Shiva's Shiva Linga to Lanka, only to place it down here, cementing it eternally to the earth.

How to Avail VIP Darshan (Sheeghra Darshanam)

Top Attractions Beyond the Main Shrine

1. Naulakha Mandir

A magnificent 146-foot white stone temple dedicated to Radha-Krishna, built in 1940 with a donation of ₹9 Lakhs by Queen Charushila of Pathuriaghat.

2. Trikut Pahar & Tapovan Caves

A striking three-peaked granite hill situated 15 km from Deoghar, famous for ancient sage meditation caves and scenic valley lookouts.

3. Basukinath Dham (42 km detour)

Pilgrimage custom holds that a visit to Baidyanath Dham is fulfilled only upon offering prayer at Basukinath, the court of Lord Shiva.
