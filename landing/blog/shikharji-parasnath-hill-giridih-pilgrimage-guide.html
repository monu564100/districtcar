Parasnath Hill (Shikharji) Giridih Pilgrimage & Trekking Guide 2026: Highest Peak of Jharkhand

District Car Travel Desk October 2026 9 min read Pilgrimage & Trekking

⚡ Shikharji Parasnath Fast Facts

Altitude: 1,365 meters (4,478 feet) — The Highest Point in Jharkhand.

Location: Madhuban, Giridih District, Eastern Jharkhand.

Spiritual Prominence: Supreme Jain Tirtha where 20 of 24 Tirthankaras attained Nirvana.

Distance from Ranchi: 165 km via Ramgarh & Bagodar (approx. 3.5 hrs drive).

Round-Trip Cab Fare: Starting at ₹2,999 with District Car.

Towering majestically above the plains of Giridih, Parasnath Hill (known globally to millions of Jain devotees as Shri Sammed Shikharji) is not only the highest geographical elevation in Jharkhand at 1,365 meters (4,478 feet), but also the most revered pilgrimage shrine in Jainism.

According to timeless Jain cosmology, 20 out of the 24 Tirthankaras attained supreme liberation (Moksha) on these holy mountain ridges. Each ridge is marked by a sacred marble temple or shrine (Tonk), culminating at the highest pinnacle dedicated to the 23rd Tirthankara, Bhagwan Parshvanatha.

The Sacred 27-Kilometer Parikrama Trek

Pilgrims typically begin the sacred yatra around 3:00 AM from the base town of Madhuban, where dozens of modern Jain Dharamshalas and bhojanshalas are located:

  • Climb to the Ridge (~9 km): A paved stone trail climbs steadily through thick sal and oak forests up to Gandharva Nala and the main ridge line.
  • Ridge Tonk Darshan (~9 km): Pilgrims perform vandana at each sacred tonk across the various peaks, including Jal Mandir and the summit tonk of Bhagwan Parshvanatha.
  • Descent to Madhuban (~9 km): Winding downward back to the base camp, completing the full 27 km circuit by late afternoon.
  • Doli / Palki Facility: For elderly pilgrims and young children, authorized local porters operate comfortable four-bearer wooden palanquins (Dolis) with pre-fixed government union rates.

How to Reach Madhuban / Parasnath

  • From Ranchi (165 km, ~3.5 hrs): Route via NH-20 past Ramgarh to Ormanjhi, then along the Grand Trunk Road (NH-19) to Bagodar and Madhuban.
  • From Parasnath Railway Station (PNME): Located just 22 km from Madhuban; District Car provides direct station pickup in under 30 minutes.
  • From Dhanbad (75 km, ~1.5 hrs): Smooth 4-lane drive along the GT Road corridor.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are pure vegetarian and Jain food available at Madhuban?

Yes. Madhuban is an entirely vegetarian holy town with dozens of institutional Jain bhojanshalas serving fresh chauvihar and navkarshi meals prepared strictly according to Jain tenets.

What is the best time of year for the Shikharji trek?

The winter months between October and March offer pleasant mountain weather (10°C–22°C), making the 27 km trek comfortable and cool.
