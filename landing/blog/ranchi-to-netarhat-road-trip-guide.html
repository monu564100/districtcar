Ranchi to Netarhat Road Trip Guide 2026: Route Map, Sunset Point & Cab Fares

District Car Travel Desk October 2026 9 min read Hill Station & Road Trip

⚡ Trip Highlights & Key Facts

Distance: 156 km from Ranchi city center via NH43 & Ghaghra.

Altitude: 1,128 meters (3,700 feet) above sea level.

Driving Time: Approx. 4 hours through smooth asphalt and forest ghats.

Recommended Duration: 2 Days / 1 Night weekend road trip package.

Perched on the highest plateau of the Pat region at 3,700 feet, Netarhat is affectionately called the "Queen of Chotanagpur". Blanketed in towering pine and eucalyptus trees, misty mountain valleys, and cool breezes all year round, Netarhat is the premier hill station retreat for travelers seeking refuge from city chaos.

The Driving Route: Ranchi to Netarhat via Ghaghra

The standard and most picturesque road route follows:

Ranchi → Bero → Ghaghra → Kudunghat → Netarhat

The first 110 km to Ghaghra is smooth 4-lane and 2-lane national highway. The final 45 km ascents into the breathtaking Kudunghat section with well-banked hairpin turns and dense Sal forest canopies.

Must-Experience Sightseeing Points

1. Magnolia Point (The Fabled Sunset Point)

Located 10 km from the main market, Magnolia Point overlooks a precipitous drop into the Chotanagpur valley. Named after a British maiden who tragic folklore says lept into the abyss on horseback, it offers India's most dramatic sunsets where the sun dips into a sea of purple forest ridges.

2. Koel View Point

A sunrise lovers' haven situated only 3 km from the bus stand. Watching the morning sun break over the winding silvery ribbon of the Koel River surrounded by morning valley mist is unforgettable.

3. Pine Forests & Upper Ghaghri Waterfall

Just 4 km outside town, Upper Ghaghri cascades amidst dense pine trees. The fragrance of pine needles and damp earth makes it a dream picnic and photography spot.

Recommended 2-Day Weekend Itinerary

  • Day 1 - 07:00 AM: Chauffeur doorstep pickup anywhere in Ranchi. Enjoy hot kachori and tea breakfast along NH43 near Bero.
  • Day 1 - 11:30 AM: Arrive in Netarhat, check in at Jharkhand Tourism Prabhat Vihar or Pine Forest Resort.
  • Day 1 - 01:30 PM: Explore Upper Ghaghri Falls and pine groves.
  • Day 1 - 04:30 PM: Arrive at Magnolia Sunset Point with piping hot tea and bhajiyas while witnessing the golden sunset.
  • Day 2 - 05:45 AM: Catch the majestic sunrise over Koel View Point.
  • Day 2 - 09:30 AM: Check out and embark on an optional detour to Lodh Falls (60 km) or return leisurely to Ranchi by evening.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Netarhat safe for night driving?

We advise completing the Kudunghat hill ascent before 07:00 PM to enjoy the scenery and avoid dense fog during winter months.

Are hotels and guest houses easily available in Netarhat?

Government resorts like Prabhat Vihar and private hotels are available, though advance weekend booking is strongly recommended during peak winter (November-February).
