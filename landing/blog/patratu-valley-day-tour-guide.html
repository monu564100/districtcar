Patratu Valley Day Tour from Ranchi: Hairpin Ghats, Speed Boating & Cab Fares

District Car Travel Desk October 2026 6 min read Weekend Day Trips

⚡ Quick Trip Summary

Distance from Ranchi: 40 km via Kanke-Pithoria Road.

Travel Duration: 1 hour drive each way.

Highlights: Serpentine hairpin ghat viewpoints, speed boating, floating restaurant, dam promenade.

Cab Fare: ₹899 round-trip (Mini) | ₹1,199 (Sedan AC) with 3 hours wait included.

If you have half a day to spare in Ranchi and want an exhilarating road trip experience, Patratu Valley is unmatched. Often compared to the winding alpine roads of Europe, this silky asphalt highway slithers down lush green hills with dramatic hairpin curves before unfurling into the sprawling blue reservoir of Patratu Dam.

Top Experiences on a Patratu Day Trip

1. The Iconic Serpentine Ghat Viewpoint

As your cab winds down the ridge, designated viewpoint laybys offer sweeping panoramic views of the entire valley. The multi-tiered loops of road glowing against the lush greenery make for postcard-perfect photography and drone shots.

2. Patratu Lake Resort & Speed Boating

The Jharkhand Tourism Development Corporation (JTDC) operates a world-class water sports complex at the dam. Visitors can enjoy:

  • Speed Boats: ₹400 for 4 people (thrilling fast spray ride across the islands).
  • Paddle Boats: ₹150 for 30 minutes (ideal for couples and serene lake drifting).
  • Jet Skis & Motor Launches: Available on weekends.

3. Lakeside Cafes & Fresh Fish Fry

Along the dam promenade, local dhabas serve freshly caught fried Rohu and Katla fish seasoned with local spices, alongside sweet corn and hot chai.

Best Half-Day Itinerary (Afternoon Sunset Tour)

  • 02:30 PM: Pickup from Ranchi (Hotel / Home / Station).
  • 03:30 PM: Halt at Patratu Ghat Viewpoint for photos and snacks.
  • 04:15 PM – 05:45 PM: Speed boating and stroll at Patratu Lake Resort.
  • 06:00 PM: Golden hour tea by the reservoir as twinkling dam lights illuminate the water.
  • 07:15 PM: Smooth return drive back into Ranchi.
