Lodh Falls (Budha Ghagh): Jharkhand's Highest Waterfall Complete Route & Cab Guide 2026

District Car Travel Desk October 2026 9 min read Latehar Tourism

⚡ Lodh Falls Essential Facts

Height: 143 meters (469 feet) — The Highest Waterfall in Jharkhand.

Location: Mahuadanr block, Latehar District (Chechhari Valley).

River: Burha River, cutting through ancient Chotanagpur granite faults.

Distance: 60 km from Netarhat, 215 km from Ranchi.

Round-Trip Cab Fare: Starting at ₹3,499 from Ranchi with District Car.

Deep in the remote, mist-cloaked forests of Latehar district, the thunderous roar of Lodh Falls (also known as Budha Ghagh) can be heard from more than 10 kilometers away. Dropping a staggering 143 meters (469 feet) into an impenetrable rocky chasm, Lodh Falls holds the crowned title of Jharkhand’s Highest Waterfall and ranks among the most magnificent tiered waterfalls in all of Asia.

Fed by the perennial Burha River traversing the lush Chechhari valley, Lodh Falls remains an awe-inspiring spectacle of sheer natural power, encircled by pristine sal forests where wild bison (gaur), deer, and colorful mountain hornbills roam undisturbed.

How to Reach Lodh Falls: Routes & Road Guide

Because Lodh Falls is located deep inside the forested plateau of Latehar, having a reliable outstation cab and an experienced hill driver is essential:

  • From Netarhat (Best Option): Netarhat → Mahuadanr → Lodh Falls. Total distance is 60 km (about 1.5 to 2 hours drive). This allows you to combine the famous Netarhat sunrise with an exhilarating afternoon waterfall safari.
  • Direct from Ranchi: Ranchi → Lohardaga → Bishunpur → Netarhat / Mahuadanr → Lodh Falls. Total distance is roughly 215 km (approx. 5 hours driving time).
  • From Daltonganj (Medininagar): Daltonganj → Latehar → Mahuadanr → Lodh Falls (~125 km).

Viewing Platforms & Steps at Lodh Falls

The Jharkhand Forest and Tourism Department has constructed paved stone pathways, safety railings, and multiple tiered viewpoints along the canyon rim:

  • Upper Watchtower: Overlooks the sheer cliff lip where the Burha River takes its thunderous leap into space.
  • Lower Viewing Deck (~80 steps descent): Brings you directly facing the tiered spray basin, offering dramatic misty backdrops for landscape photography.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the best month to visit Lodh Falls?

September to February is the optimal season. The post-monsoon water volume is massive, the surrounding Chechhari valley is lush green, and the winter climate is refreshingly cool.

Are permits required to visit Lodh Falls?

A nominal entry fee (₹20 per person) and vehicle parking fee are paid at the forest checkpoint near the entrance. No special advance permit is required.
