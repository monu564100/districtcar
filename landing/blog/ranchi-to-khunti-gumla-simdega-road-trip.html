Ranchi to Khunti, Gumla & Simdega Road Trip 2026: The Ultimate Southern Jharkhand Circuit

District Car Travel Desk October 2026 10 min read Road Trips

⚡ Road Trip Fast Facts

Total Driving Distance: ~420 km loop across 4 districts (Ranchi, Khunti, Simdega, Gumla).

Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights.

Highlights: Panchghagh Falls, Torpa Perwaghagh, Kolebira, Kelaghagh Dam, Palkot Sanctuary, Anjan Dham, and Nagfeni Palace.

Recommended Cab: Prime Sedan or SUV booked through District Car.

If you are looking for an expansive road trip that combines lush Sal tree plateaus, roaring waterfalls, serene hill dams, Ramayana mythology, and the warm hospitality of indigenous tribal cultures, the Southern Jharkhand Circuit is the gold standard.

This complete 3-day itinerary takes you out of Ranchi along the smooth NH-20 and NH-43 highways, weaving through Khunti, Torpa, Kolebira, Simdega, Palkot, and Gumla before returning smoothly to Ranchi.

Complete 3-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Ranchi to Khunti, Panchghagh Falls & Simdega

  • 08:00 AM: Doorstep pickup in Ranchi by your District Car chauffeur. Cruise down the 4-lane NH-20 to Khunti.
  • 09:30 AM: Stop at Deer Park Kalamati and explore Amreshwar Dham (Angrabari Temple).
  • 11:30 AM: Arrive at Panchghagh Falls. Enjoy peaceful walks by the 5 gentle streams and have a relaxed picnic lunch.
  • 02:00 PM: Drive through Torpa and Kolebira along state highways surrounded by dense forest thickets.
  • 05:00 PM: Arrive in Simdega. Check into your hotel and take an evening boat ride on Kelaghagh Dam as the sunset paints the water gold.

Day 2: Simdega to Palkot Wildlife Sanctuary & Gumla

  • 08:30 AM: Enjoy a hearty breakfast of local hot kachori and jalebi in Simdega market.
  • 10:00 AM: Drive north towards Palkot Wildlife Sanctuary. Explore the majestic boulder formations, King Sugriva's Cave, and the ice-cold natural water springs of Sheetalpur.
  • 02:00 PM: Head to Gumla town for lunch (taste authentic Jharkhandi mutton curry or desi saag-bhat).
  • 03:30 PM: Visit Anjan Dham, the sacred hilltop birthplace of Lord Hanuman. Trek to the cave shrine and soak in the sunset across the Chotanagpur hills.
  • Overnight: Stay at Gumla.

Day 3: Gumla, Nagfeni Palace, Sisai & Return to Ranchi

  • 09:00 AM: Explore Basudevkona shrines and take photographs of the medieval stone carvings.
  • 11:00 AM: Drive along NH-43 to Nagfeni Palace ruins. Marvel at the ancient Nagvanshi stone architecture and the Cobra rock formation overlooking the river.
  • 01:30 PM: Traditional lunch stop at Bero dhaba corridor (famous for hot dhuska and chana ghugni).
  • 04:30 PM: Arrive comfortably back at your home or hotel in Ranchi.
