Why Palkot Village Gumla Guide 2026 is Special

Explore Palkot village in Gumla — ancient capital of the Nagvanshi dynasty, Sugriva Cave of Ramayana Kishkindha lore, Gobar Silli rocking rock, Bawlis & District Car cab fares.

Palkot is an awe-inspiring eco-heritage village nestled amid rocky boulder hills and Sal forests in Gumla. Historically the royal capital of the Chotanagpur Nagvanshi Maharajas in the 18th century, folklore also associates Palkot's caves with King Sugriva and Bali of Ramayana's Kishkindha.

How to Reach Palkot Block, Gumla District

  • From Ranchi (110 km): Ranchi → Bero → Gumla → Palkot (2.5 hrs via NH-43). Well-maintained 2-lane state highway winding through forest villages.
  • From Gumla Town (25 km): Palkot is located just 25 km south of Gumla district headquarters on the Gumla-Simdega route.
  • From Simdega (55 km): Simdega → Kolebira → Palkot (1 hr via NH-143).
  • Best Visiting Season: October through March. Winter mornings bring mist-shrouded boulder summits and pleasant trekking temperatures.

Key Attractions & Village Highlights

Sugriva Gufa & Ramayana Kishkindha Caverns

Explore the deep natural cave labyrinth where tribal folklore believes King Sugriva took shelter. A perennial cold mountain spring flows inside the cave even in mid-summer.

Nagvanshi Royal Palace Ruins & Stepwells (Bawli)

Walk through 250-year-old fort ramparts, stone watchtowers, and the grand 'Sheetalpur Nawakhana' stepwells built by Maharaja Harinath Sahi Deo in 1772.

Gobar Silli Natural Rocking Boulder

Visit the geological marvel of Gobar Silli — a giant spherical granite boulder balancing precariously on a hill slope that rocks when pushed with one hand yet never falls.

Palkot Wildlife Sanctuary Biodiversity

Home to sloth bears, leopards, pangolins, and rare medicinal plants used in tribal Ayurvedic traditions.

Local Food & Stay Options

Palkot village features local dhabas serving hot dehati dal-bhat, chokha, and local river fish. Stay options include Palkot Forest Rest House or standard hotels in Gumla town (25 km away).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is Palkot considered connected to Ramayana?

Local folklore and oral tribal traditions identify Palkot's rocky caverns, Matanga Vapi stepwells, and Rishyamukha hills with the kingdom of Kishkindha described in the Ramayana where Lord Rama met Sugriva.

What is the best way to reach Palkot from Ranchi?

The most convenient way is booking an outstation round-trip cab on District Car via NH-43. The distance is 110 km and takes 2.5 hours.

Is trekking inside Sugriva Cave safe?

Yes, local village guides are available at the entrance who carry torches. The path has stone steps, but wear shoes with rubber grip as cave rocks can be damp.

Can we combine Palkot with Anjan Dham and Tanginath?

Yes, Gumla forms an incredible heritage triangle: Palkot (Sugriva Gufa) → Anjan Dham (Hanuman Birthplace) → Tanginath (Ancient Trishul). A 2-day SUV tour is highly recommended.
