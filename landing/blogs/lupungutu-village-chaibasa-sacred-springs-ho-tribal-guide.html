Why Lupungutu Village Chaibasa Guide 2026 is Special

Visit Lupungutu village near Chaibasa in West Singhbhum. Natural cold perennial bubbling springs, ancient Sal sacred groves (Jaher), Ho tribal lifestyle & District Car cab fares.

Lupungutu is an idyllic indigenous Ho tribal village just 2 km from Chaibasa town. 'Lupungutu' in the native Ho language translates literally to 'bubbling spring of crystal water'. Beneath deep groves of giant Sal trees, natural subterranean springs bubble out 365 days a year with sweet, crystal-clear mineral water.

How to Reach Lupungutu, Chaibasa, West Singhbhum

  • From Chaibasa Town (2.5 km): Just a 5-minute drive from Chaibasa railway station or bus terminal.
  • From Jamshedpur (65 km): Jamshedpur → Hata → Chaibasa → Lupungutu (1.5 hrs via State Highway 5).
  • From Ranchi (145 km): Ranchi → Khunti → Bandgaon Ghat → Chaibasa → Lupungutu (3.5 hrs via NH-20 & SH-5).
  • Best Visiting Season: Throughout the year, especially winter (November to February) and spring during Baha festival (March-April).

Key Attractions & Village Highlights

Perennial Natural Bubbling Water Springs

Witness sweet groundwater gushing out of stone crevices inside a peaceful grove. The water remains refreshingly cool in peak summer and pleasantly warm in winter.

Authentic Ho Tribal Lifestyle & Architecture

Observe pristine courtyard homes painted with tribal geometric motifs, where the indigenous Ho people live in close harmony with sacred nature.

Jaher Sthan Sacred Groves

Learn about the ancient tribal religious sanctuary where Sarhul and Baha flower ceremonies are held under blossoming Sal canopies.

Chaibasa Weekly Haat & Tribal Handicrafts

Visit the bustling weekly village market to buy bell-metal items, handloom tribal shawls, forest honey, and authentic bamboo craft.

Local Food & Stay Options

Chaibasa town offers excellent local eateries serving hot litti-chokha, desi sweets, and tribal rice delicacies. Clean family hotels and government circuit houses are available in Chaibasa (2 km).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the meaning of Lupungutu?

In the Ho indigenous tribal language, 'Lupun' signifies bubbling spring water and 'Gutu' means a hillock or grove. Together it means 'The Grove of Bubbling Springs'.

Is the spring water at Lupungutu safe to drink?

Yes, the groundwater emerges directly from natural mineral fissures and local villagers and visitors drink it fresh for its natural purity and digestive properties.

What are the road conditions from Ranchi to Chaibasa?

The route via NH-20 till Chakradharpur/Bandgaon is scenic. District Car's experienced local drivers navigate the winding Bandgaon forest ghats safely with fixed rates.

Can we combine Lupungutu with Saranda Forest?

Yes, Lupungutu is on the gateway route to the massive Saranda Sal forest and Kiriburu hill station. A 2-day District Car outstation tour covers both.
