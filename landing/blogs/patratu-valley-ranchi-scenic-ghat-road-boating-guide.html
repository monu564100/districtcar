Why Patratu Valley Is Jharkhand's Most Photographed Drive

40 km from Ranchi, Patratu Valley is where the Ranchi plateau (1,800 ft) drops 1,200 ft to the Patratu Dam reservoir via a 8 km ghat road with 12 hairpin bends — each numbered and painted. From Bend 6 Hilltop (near Jharkhand Tourism Hilltop Resort), the valley unfolds as a green S-curve with Patratu Dam lake glinting below and Ramgarh hills beyond.

Uniqueness: it's Jharkhand's only hill ghat that ends at a dam lake — you drive curves at the top, boat at the bottom, and sunset at hilltop in one trip. Bikers, car clubs, and drone photographers treat it as Jharkhand's 'mini Lonavala ghat' — but with a dam, not a city. Monsoon (Jul-Sep) valley turns emerald; winter (Nov-Feb) lake is mirror-clear.

How to Reach Patratu Valley & Dam

  • From Ranchi (40 km): Ranchi → Kanke → Pithoria → Patratu ghat (12 bends) → Patratu Dam (1.2 hrs). Road: 4-lane till Pithoria (NH-23), then ghat single — start 6:30 AM for mist-free valley photo at hilltop.
  • From Ramgarh (25 km): Ramgarh → Patratu Road (40 min, flat, good).
  • Viewpoint & parking: Hilltop Resort lawn (₹20 parking, viewpoint free, tea at resort). Dam base parking (₹30/car).
  • Timing: Viewpoint best 7-9 AM (mist lifts) & 4:30-5:30 PM (sunset over dam). Boating 9 AM - 5:30 PM.

Top Things to Do in Patratu Valley

1. Hilltop Viewpoint — 12 Bends Photo Point

Bend 6 Hilltop: tripod, wide lens (18mm), capture S-curves + dam. Drone allowed (below 120m, no resort privacy violation).

2. Patratu Dam Boating — Lake Below Ghat

Motor/jet ski on reservoir with valley hills behind — evening water pink at sunset. Hilltop resort overlooks boating lake.

3. Ghat Drive — 12 Curves Slow Experience

Drive 8 km ghat at 20 kmph, horns at bends, windows down for Sal + mahua forest scent. No race — police speed check at Bend 3.

4. Combine: Patratu (AM) → McCluskieganj Bungalows (PM, 10 km) → Ranchi

Patratu valley (7 AM) → McCluskieganj heritage bungalows + Jackhills (11 AM) — valley + Anglo-Indian village in one District Car SUV day.

Food & Stay

Hilltop Resort restaurant (thali ₹150, tea, pakora, valley view) + Patratu Dam stalls (maggi, omelette). Stay: Hilltop Resort (₹2,500/night, valley view AC) or return to Ranchi (day trip). District Car driver knows viewpoint tea stop (pre-call).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How many curves in Patratu Valley ghat road?

12 numbered hairpin bends (U-turns) descending from Kanke-Pithoria plateau (1,800 ft) to Patratu Dam (600 ft) over 8 km. Viewpoint at Bend 6 (Hilltop Resort) gives the classic S-curve valley photo with dam in distance — reach before 9 AM for mist.

What are Patratu Dam boating charges?

Pedal boat ₹100, motor ₹250, jet ski ₹400 (10 min, 9 AM - 5:30 PM). Dam entry free (6 AM - 6 PM). Resort hilltop tea (₹50) while viewing valley is a ritual.

How far is Patratu Valley from Ranchi?

40 km from Ranchi (Birsa Chowk) via Kanke-Pithoria ghat road (1.2 hrs, excellent 4-lane till Pithoria, then 8 km ghat). Road: 4-lane till Pithoria, then single ghat with 12 bends — drive slow, horn at curves, no overtaking.
