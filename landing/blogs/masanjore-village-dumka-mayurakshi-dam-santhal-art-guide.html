Why Masanjore Village Dumka Guide 2026 is Special

Explore Masanjore village in Dumka (Santhal Pargana). Historic 1955 Canadian Dam on Mayurakshi river, Peacock Island, boat rides, Santhal village murals & District Car cab fares.

Masanjore is an enchanting lakeside village nestled between lush rolling forest hills on the banks of the Mayurakshi River in Dumka. Known for the historic 1955 Canada Dam, its massive turquoise-blue reservoir, picturesque islands, and vibrant Santhal tribal settlements make it Eastern Jharkhand's premier leisure destination.

How to Reach Masanjore, Dumka District, Santhal Pargana

  • From Dumka Town (35 km): Dumka → Ranibahal → Masanjore (40 min via smooth state highway).
  • From Deoghar (95 km): Deoghar → Dumka → Masanjore (2 hrs via NH-114A). Perfect day excursion after Baba Baidyanath darshan.
  • From Ranchi (275 km): Ranchi → Bokaro → Dhanbad → Jamtara → Dumka → Masanjore (6 hrs).
  • Best Visiting Season: October through March. Winter skies turn the water deep azure blue, and local boaters operate trips to rocky islands.

Key Attractions & Village Highlights

The 1955 Canada Dam Marvel

Walk across the 2,170-foot-long heritage masonry dam built with Canadian international cooperation, offering panoramic vistas of endless blue waters.

Island Boating to Peacock Island (Mayur Dweep)

Take speedboats or wooden country boats across the reservoir to secluded green islands dotted with migratory winter birds.

Santhali Tribal Wall Murals & Homestay Culture

Wander through adjacent Santhal village hamlets where every clay house is lovingly adorned with floral patterns, bull carts, and tribal dancing figures.

Freshwater River Fish Delicacies

Enjoy freshly caught Mayurakshi fish (Katla, Rohu) prepared in mustard oil with desi spices at lakeside family eateries.

Local Food & Stay Options

Lakeside eateries serve mouthwatering fresh fish thalis, rice, and hot parathas. Stay at the Mayurakshi Tourist Lodge, DVC Inspection Bungalow, or quality hotels in Dumka city (35 km).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is Masanjore Dam called Canada Dam?

The dam was completed in 1955 with financial and engineering assistance under the Colombo Plan from the Government of Canada, inaugurated by Canadian Foreign Minister Lester B. Pearson.

How far is Masanjore from Deoghar?

Masanjore is 95 km east of Deoghar (around 2 hours drive). Pilgrims visiting Deoghar Baba Baidyanath Mandir frequently book District Car for a peaceful Masanjore day excursion.

Is boating available at Masanjore Dam?

Yes, licensed speedboats and pedal boats operate from 8:00 AM to 5:30 PM. Safety life jackets are provided.

What is the cab fare from Deoghar or Dumka to Masanjore?

From Dumka, round-trip rates start at ₹2,199 (Mini) and ₹2,699 (Sedan). From Deoghar, round-trip SUV cabs are available at ₹4,499 on District Car.
