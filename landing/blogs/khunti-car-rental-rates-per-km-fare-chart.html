Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Are there any hidden charges like booking fees or convenience charges?

No! District Car never charges platform convenience fees to customers. The price you see is the exact price you pay directly to the driver.

How does the 35% round-trip discount work?

When you book a two-way journey (returning to your pickup location within the same day), our pricing engine automatically deducts 35% from the return leg fare, making round-trips highly affordable.

Are toll taxes and parking fees included in the per-km rate?

State highway toll tickets and local parking charges are payable as actuals by the passenger during the trip.

Can I rent a car for wedding functions in Khunti?

Yes! District Car offers dedicated decorated wedding car packages for brides and grooms, as well as multi-vehicle convoy fleets for baratis.
