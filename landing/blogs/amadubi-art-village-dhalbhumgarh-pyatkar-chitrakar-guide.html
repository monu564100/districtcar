Why Amadubi Village Guide 2026 is Special

Discover Amadubi (Village of Painters) in Dhalbhumgarh near Jamshedpur. Traditional Pyatkar scroll art, Santhal folk music, mud painted houses, eco-resort & District Car cab fares.

Amadubi is India's acclaimed 'Village of Chitrakars' (Pyatkar scroll painters). For generations, indigenous artisans have made organic herbal colors from forest stones and leaves to paint Santhal epics on handmade paper scrolls. Stroll through mud houses adorned with Sohrai murals and experience authentic Santhali homestays.

How to Reach Amadubi, Dhalbhumgarh, East Singhbhum

  • From Jamshedpur (65 km): Jamshedpur → Ghatshila → Dhalbhumgarh → Amadubi (1.5 hrs via NH-18). Smooth 4-lane highway till Dhalbhumgarh, then scenic village road.
  • From Ranchi (185 km): Ranchi → Bundu → Chandil → Jamshedpur bypass → Dhalbhumgarh (4 hrs via NH-33 & NH-18).
  • Nearest Railway Station: Dhalbhumgarh Station (8 km) or Ghatshila Station (18 km). Regular auto and District Car taxi service available.
  • Best Timing: October to March. Winter art fairs, weekly tribal haats on Tuesdays, and evening Sarpa folk dance performances.

Key Attractions & Village Highlights

Live Pyatkar Scroll Painting Demonstration

Watch 5th-generation Chitrakar artists extract sap from neem bark, turmeric, and river clay to paint intricate scroll scrolls narrating local tribal lore.

Santhali Tribal Mud Architecture

Admire clean village paths flanked by adobe houses with vibrant red, black, and ochre murals depicting peacocks, deer, and sacred sal groves.

Amadubi Rural Eco-Tourism Cottage Complex

Stay in authentic thatched ethnic huts equipped with clean amenities, managed directly by the village tourism committee.

Folk Dance & Mahali Bamboo Craft

Enjoy evening Sarpa and Dasai dance with Madal drums and purchase handwoven bamboo baskets and flutes directly from rural artisans.

Local Food & Stay Options

Enjoy organic village meals: Country chicken curry (Desi Murga), Marua roti, Dudh Pitha, and wild forest greens (Gundli saag). Stay at the Amadubi Rural Tourism Centre (₹1,200–₹2,000/night) or book a day return trip from Jamshedpur hotels with District Car.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What makes Amadubi Pyatkar art so famous?

Pyatkar is one of the oldest scroll-painting traditions of India, practiced by the Chitrakar community of Amadubi. The scrolls narrate oral tribal folklore and history using 100% natural organic dyes made from forest rocks, leaves, and bark.

How far is Amadubi from Jamshedpur?

Amadubi is roughly 65 km southeast of Jamshedpur via NH-18 (Dhalbhumgarh route). The drive takes around 1 hour 30 minutes in a District Car cab.

Can we buy original paintings directly from artists?

Yes! You can purchase authentic framed scrolls and paper artwork directly from the artists' homes starting from ₹300 to ₹5,000, ensuring direct livelihood support to rural families.
