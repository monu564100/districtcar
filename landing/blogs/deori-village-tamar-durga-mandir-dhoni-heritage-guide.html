Why Deori Village Tamar Guide 2026 is Special

Plan your trip to Deori Village in Tamar on NH-33 near Ranchi. Discover the 700-year-old 16-armed stone Durga temple, MS Dhoni pilgrimage, tribal Pahan customs & District Car fares.

Deori village in Tamar is famous across Eastern India for the ancient 700-year-old Maa Deori Temple. Unlike standard temples, here an exquisite 16-armed black stone idol of Goddess Durga is worshipped, and rituals are performed jointly by tribal Pahan priests alongside Brahmin priests.

How to Reach Deori, Tamar Block, Ranchi District

  • From Ranchi (60 km): Ranchi → Bundu → Tamar → Deori Mandir (1.2 hrs via NH-33 4-lane highway). Excellent road condition with multiple food stops.
  • From Jamshedpur (70 km): Jamshedpur → Chandil → Chowka → Tamar → Deori (1.4 hrs via NH-33).
  • Nearest Railway Station: Ranchi Junction (60 km) or Muri Junction (45 km).
  • Best Timing: Open 5:30 AM to 8:30 PM year-round. Tuesdays, Navratri festivals, and Makar Sankranti witness divine celebrations.

Key Attractions & Village Highlights

700-Year-Old Solha-Bhuji (16-Armed) Durga Idol

Marvel at the 3-foot stone deity with 16 arms holding traditional weapons, believed to have been consecrated by a 14th-century Nagvanshi ruler.

Unique Tribal Pahan & Brahmin Synchronous Worship

For 6 days a week, tribal Pahan priests conduct rituals according to ancient Santhal/Munda customs, and Brahmin priests lead puja on Tuesdays.

The Mahendra Singh Dhoni Connection

Cricket legend MS Dhoni has visited Deori Temple before every major world tournament and milestone match, praying at this rustic roadside village sanctum.

Rural Tamar Organic Lac & Sweets Belt

Tamar and Bundu villages are renowned for golden Khoya pedas and organic lac farming. Stop by the highway stalls for hot jalebis and fresh chhena sweets.

Local Food & Stay Options

Deori village features numerous fresh vegetarian eateries, sweet shops serving hot Bundu pedas and kachori-sabzi. For luxury stay, return to Ranchi (1.2 hrs away) with your District Car cab.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is Deori Temple idol having 16 arms?

Traditional Durga idols have 8 or 10 arms. The Deori idol has 16 arms holding bow, shield, sword, trident, conch, and chakra, representing supreme vanquishing power over all obstacles.

How far is Deori Village from Ranchi city?

It is exactly 60 km from Ranchi Railway Station on the smooth 4-lane Ranchi-Tata Highway (NH-33). The drive takes approximately 1 hour 15 minutes.

Can we combine Deori temple with Bundu Sun Temple?

Yes! Bundu Sun Temple is just 18 km before Deori. Most District Car riders book a combo tour covering Bundu Sun Temple, Dassam Falls, and Deori Temple on the same day.
