Why Gumo Village Koderma Guide 2026 is Special

Plan a visit to Gumo village and Tilaiya Dam in Koderma. DVC's first reservoir on Barakar river, island boat rides, organic tomato belt, fish cuisine & District Car cab fares.

Gumo and surrounding villages flank the historic Tilaiya Dam — the very first hydro-electric dam built by the Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC) in 1953 across the Barakar River. Flanked by verdant hillocks, Gumo combines rich freshwater fishery cooperatives, thriving organic tomato belts, and lakeside serenity.

How to Reach Gumo / Tilaiya Dam, Koderma District

  • From Jhumri Telaiya Town (18 km): Jhumri Telaiya → Barhi Road → Tilaiya Dam / Gumo (25 min via NH-20 & Dam Road).
  • From Ranchi (160 km): Ranchi → Ramgarh → Hazaribagh → Barhi → Tilaiya Dam (3.5 hrs via NH-20 4-lane).
  • From Hazaribagh (65 km): Hazaribagh → Barhi → Tilaiya Dam (1 hr 15 min via NH-20).
  • Best Season: October to March. Cool breezes blow over the 36 sq km reservoir, making island picnics and speed boating delightful.

Key Attractions & Village Highlights

DVC's First 1953 Concrete Gravity Dam

Walk along the 1,200-foot concrete dam crest enjoying breathtaking views of the sparkling lake surrounded by the rugged Parasnath hill range.

Reservoir Island Boating & Water Sports

Board motorboats from Urwan or Gumo boating docks to explore uninhabited green reservoir islands and birdwatching sanctuaries.

Gumo Organic Tomato & Vegetable Belt

Koderma's Gumo belt is renowned for sweet hybrid tomatoes and seasonal vegetables grown using canal drip irrigation and exported across eastern India.

Tilaiya Dam Fresh Barakar Fish Cuisine

Savor hot freshly-caught Barakar river fish fried with local spices served with steamed rice and tangy tomato chutney at lakeside dhabas.

Local Food & Stay Options

Taste fresh river fish (Tengra, Rohu, Mirgal) and famous Jhumri Telaiya Kalakand sweet. Stay options include DVC Guest House, Urwan Tourist Complex, or comfortable hotels in Jhumri Telaiya town (18 km).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the history of Tilaiya Dam?

Inaugurated in 1953 by Prime Minister Jawaharlal Nehru, Tilaiya Dam was the first multipurpose reservoir built under the Damodar Valley Corporation (DVC) to tame floodwaters and generate clean hydroelectric power.

How far is Tilaiya Dam from Ranchi and Patna?

Tilaiya Dam is 160 km north of Ranchi via NH-20 (3.5 hrs) and roughly 185 km south of Patna via Nawada/Barhi highway.

Is boating available for tourists?

Yes, the state tourism department and local boatmen operate speedboats, family motorboats, and paddle boats from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM daily.
