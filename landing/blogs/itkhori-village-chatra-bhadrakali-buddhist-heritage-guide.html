Why Itkhori Village Chatra Guide 2026 is Special

Discover Itkhori village in Chatra, a sacred tri-junction of Hinduism, Buddhism & Jainism on Mohane River. Maa Bhadrakali temple, 1008-lingam pillar, stupas & District Car cab fares.

Itkhori is an extraordinary archaeological and pilgrimage village nestled at the scenic confluence of the Mohane and Baksa rivers in Chatra. It is revered as a sacred tri-junction where Hindu, Buddhist, and Jain faiths converge harmoniously amidst ancient stone temples and monastic excavations.

How to Reach Itkhori Block, Chatra District

  • From Hazaribagh (60 km): Hazaribagh → Padma → Chouparan → Itkhori (1.2 hrs via NH-19 & SH).
  • From Ranchi (150 km): Ranchi → Ramgarh → Hazaribagh → Itkhori (3.5 hrs via NH-20 4-lane).
  • From Bodh Gaya, Bihar (75 km): Bodh Gaya → Dobhi → Chouparan → Itkhori (1.5 hrs).
  • Best Visiting Season: October to March. The annual Itkhori Mahotsav in February brings cultural performances from across Eastern India.

Key Attractions & Village Highlights

Maa Bhadrakali 9th-Century Stone Temple

Worship at the sanctum housing a black basalt idol of Goddess Bhadrakali seated on a lotus, sculpted during the Pala empire.

Gautam Buddha's 'Iti Khoyi' Enlightenment Lore

Buddhist legend holds that Prince Siddhartha's aunt came looking for him here, saying 'Iti Khoyi' (I have lost him here), giving the village its name. Marvel at ancient Ashokan stupas and Tara sculptures.

Birthplace of 10th Jain Tirthankara Bhagwan Sheetalnath

Jain pilgrims revere Bhadali/Itkhori as the sacred birthplace of Sheetalnath, marked by ancient footprints (Charan Paduka) and idols.

Monumental Sahasralinga (1008 Shivalingas in One Pillar)

Witness a magnificent monolithic stone Shivalinga carved with 1,008 miniature lingas on its cylindrical body.

Local Food & Stay Options

Local dhabas serve wholesome North Indian vegetarian meals, sattu parathas, and fresh sweets. Stay at the tourist guest house in Itkhori or hotels in Hazaribagh (60 km).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is Itkhori considered a tri-religion pilgrimage?

Itkhori is unique in India as it holds immense sanctity for Hindus (Maa Bhadrakali & Sahasralinga), Buddhists (Buddha's meditation site & Tara statues), and Jains (birthplace of 10th Tirthankara Sheetalnath).

How far is Itkhori from Ranchi and Bodh Gaya?

Itkhori is 150 km north of Ranchi (3.5 hrs) and just 75 km south of Bodh Gaya (1.5 hrs), making it an essential stop on the Buddhist heritage circuit.
