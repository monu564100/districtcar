## Hydrological Details & Geological Evolution

### The Chotanagpur Plateau Fault Line
Jharkhand’s waterfalls are geologically classified as **knickpoint waterfalls** formed during tectonic uplifts of the ancient Gondwanaland shield. When river beds crossing the higher Ranchi and Netarhat erosion surfaces (2,000–3,700 ft) suddenly cross major fault planes dropping down toward the Gangetic or Subarnarekha plains, sharp vertical gorges and foaming cascades are created.

### Seasonal Water Flow Cycles
1. **Monsoon Rush (July to October):** High water volume, misty spray clouds, intense roar, vibrant green foliage. Caution is required near river edges.
2. **Winter Clarity (November to February):** Crisp weather, clear turquoise and emerald plunge pools, ideal for picnics, walking on granite flats, and photography.
3. **Summer Slumber (March to June):** Reduced water flow, tranquil river beds, cooler temperatures in the forest shade.
