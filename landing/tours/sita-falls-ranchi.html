About Sita Falls (Radha Fall & Canyon) — Travel Overview

Sita Falls, also locally known as Radha Falls, is an ethereal, lesser-known waterfall tucked deep inside the deciduous forests near Gautamdhara (Jonha) in Ranchi district. Fed by a perennial tributary of the Kanchi River, the stream drops over 150 feet through a dramatic series of polished black rock steps before terminating in an emerald-clear mountain pool. Surrounded by sheer rocky amphitheaters and dense green canopies, Sita Falls offers absolute tranquility away from commercial tourist noise. A scenic descent of around 350 stone steps brings trekkers right to the mist-drenched foot of the cascade.

Road Route & Driving Directions

Take the Ranchi-Purulia Road (NH-320) past Angara and turn towards Jonha. A dedicated forest link road leads visitors right to the Sita Falls parking lot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit both Jonha Falls and Sita Falls on the same trip?

Absolutely! Sita Falls is just 5 km from Jonha Falls. District Car’s Jonha-Sita combo cab covers both waterfalls at a discounted round-trip fare of ₹1,499.

Is the trek down to Sita Falls difficult for elderly people?

There are around 350 stone stairs. While descending is easy, climbing back up requires moderate fitness. Resting benches are provided along the trail.
