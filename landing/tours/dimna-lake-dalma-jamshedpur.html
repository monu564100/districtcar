About Dimna Lake & Dalma Foothills — Travel Overview

Dimna Lake is an enchanting artificial reservoir constructed by Tata Steel in the 1940s at the foot of the iconic Dalma Mountain Range in Jamshedpur. The sprawling reservoir is encircled by lush, rolling sal hills that host wild elephants and deer within the adjoining Dalma Sanctuary. Early morning mist rising from the calm, glass-like lake surface creates an unforgettable postcard view. Families, fitness enthusiasts, and nature photographers flock to Dimna Lake for lakeside cycling, boating, and winter picnics beneath the towering mountain shadows.

How far is Dimna Lake from Jamshedpur Railway Station (Tatanagar)?

Dimna Lake is approximately 16 km from Tatanagar Junction, taking roughly 30 minutes by cab.

Can I visit Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary from Dimna Lake?

Yes! The main entrance gate to Dalma Wildlife Sanctuary is just 7 km from Dimna Lake, making it easy to cover both in a single day trip.
