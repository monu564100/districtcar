About Jonha Falls (Gautamdhara)

Jonha Falls is a classic hanging valley cascade where the holy Raru River drops 43 meters (141 ft). Perched atop the plateau stands a historic Lord Buddha Ashram and Shiva Temple where tradition holds Gautama Buddha bathed. A 700-step stone stairway descends down through lush sal forest to the foaming river basin.
