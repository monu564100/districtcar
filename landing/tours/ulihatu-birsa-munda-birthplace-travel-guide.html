Overview & Route Highlights

Ulihatu, located in the Arki block of Khunti district, is one of the most revered historical and cultural pilgrimage destinations in India. This pristine hamlet surrounded by dense sal forests and the rolling Chotanagpur hills is the birthplace of **Dharti Aaba Bhagwan Birsa Munda** (born November 15, 1875), who led the legendary *Ulgulan* (The Great Tumult) against British colonial oppression. Visitors to Ulihatu can witness the preserved ancestral home of Birsa Munda, the magnificent bronze memorial statue, the dedicated Birsa Munda Memorial Complex, and interact with the proud Munda tribal community. With District Car's dedicated door-to-door cab service from Ranchi and Khunti Sadar, travellers can embark on a comfortable and enriching heritage pilgrimage with experienced local drivers who know every curve of the hill route.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How far is Ulihatu from Ranchi and what is the best route?

Ulihatu is approximately 65 km southeast of Ranchi. The best route is via Ranchi-Khunti 4-lane highway (NH-75E), turning toward Saiko and Arki. The total drive time is roughly 1 hour 45 minutes.

Can we book a same-day return cab to Ulihatu from Ranchi?

Yes! District Car provides dedicated full-day return packages where the cab waits for you at Ulihatu and drops you back safely. Flat 35% discount applies on the return segment.

Is the road to Ulihatu suitable for sedan and hatchback cars?

Yes, the road from Khunti to Saiko and onwards to Ulihatu is fully paved with blacktop tarmac. District Car hatchbacks, sedans, and SUVs navigate the route smoothly.

Are food and refreshments available at Ulihatu?

Basic snacks, fresh tender coconut, and tea stalls are available near the memorial complex. For full meals, Saiko Chowk or Khunti town offers family restaurants and traditional dhuskas.
