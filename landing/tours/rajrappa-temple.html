About Rajrappa Temple — The Sacred Tantric Seat

Situated in Ramgarh district of Jharkhand, the Chhinnamastika Temple at Rajrappa is revered as one of the most powerful and auspicious Hindu shrines in Eastern India. Here, the Goddess Chhinnamasta—one of the ten Mahavidyas—stands over Kama and Rati on a lotus bed.

The temple is geographically unique: it is perched at the scenic cliff edge where the roaring Bhairavi (Bhera) River plunges into the tranquil Damodar River below, forming a picturesque waterfall and natural sangam. Pilgrims take holy dips in the river before offering red silk chunris, coconuts, and traditional peda prasad.
