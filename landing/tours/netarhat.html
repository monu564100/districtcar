About Netarhat — The Serene Plateau of Jharkhand

Perched at a cool 3,700 feet above sea level in Latehar district, Netarhat is universally celebrated as the "Queen of Chotanagpur". While the plains of eastern India can get warm and humid, Netarhat retains a crisp, invigorating mountain climate year-round.

Sponsored Ad • प्रायोजित विज्ञापन

The hill station is famous for its romantic Magnolia Sunset Point, where British legend speaks of a maiden named Magnolia who rode her horse off the cliff for the love of a local shepherd. As the sun sets behind the endless undulating blue ridges, the sky turns vibrant orange and crimson. The aromatic scent of pine needles, morning mist hanging over Koel River valley, and tranquil pear orchards make it a must-visit road trip destination.

Must-Visit Attractions in Netarhat

  • Magnolia Point: Iconic sunset viewing point offering an unobstructed 180-degree view over rolling sal and teak valley ridges.
  • Pine Forest: Thick towering pine trees and soft carpeted needle pathways, ideal for nature photography and peaceful strolls.
  • Upper Ghaghri Waterfall: A picturesque 4-meter drop hidden 4 km inside the forest, perfect for quiet family picnics.
  • Koel River Viewpoint: Panoramic morning sunrise lookout where the winding Koel river shimmers under morning fog.
  • Netarhat Residential School: Historic institution founded in 1954 known for producing top Indian civil servants and scholars.

Is it better to do a same-day trip or an overnight stay in Netarhat?

While same-day round-trips from Ranchi are popular (starting early at 7:00 AM and returning after sunset), an overnight stay is strongly recommended so you can enjoy both the famous sunset and the mesmerizing morning sunrise over the Koel River.
