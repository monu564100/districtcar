About Baba Amreshwar Dham (Angrabari) — Travel Overview

Baba Amreshwar Dham, reverently known as Angrabari, is one of the most prominent spiritual sanctuaries in Jharkhand, located 9 km southwest of Khunti along the Torpa road. The temple houses a self-manifested (Swayambhu) Shivling beneath ancient banyan and mango trees. Renamed "Amreshwar Dham" by the revered Hindu seer Swami Swaroopanand Saraswati, the serene complex also enshrines temples dedicated to Lord Rama, Sita, Hanuman, and Goddess Durga. During the holy month of Shravan, tens of thousands of Kanwariyas gather to offer holy water to Lord Shiva. A grand ₹15-crore temple corridor is currently under development.
