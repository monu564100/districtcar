About Kelaghagh Dam & Waterfall — Travel Overview

Kelaghagh Dam is a picturesque water reservoir nestled among undulating green hillocks just 4 km from Simdega town, celebrated as the cradle of Indian hockey. The dam is built on the Chhinda River, and during high water seasons, the surplus overflow rushes over granite ledges forming the graceful Kelaghagh Waterfall. Surrounded by whispering eucalyptus and pine plantations, the reservoir offers pedal boating, speedboating, and a tranquil lakeside park. The peaceful, pollution-free atmosphere makes it a prized retreat for families travelling through South Chotanagpur.
