About Baba Baidyanath Dham — The Divine Sanctuary of Deoghar

Baba Baidyanath Temple, colloquially known as Baidyanath Dham, is one of the revered 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva in India and is uniquely recognized as one of the 51 Shaktipeeths as well (where Mother Sati’s heart is believed to have fallen).

According to Hindu scripture, the demon king Ravana worshipped Lord Shiva here to obtain an invincible lingam to take to Lanka. Lord Shiva granted his boon on the condition that if Ravana set the lingam on the earth anywhere en-route, it would remain rooted forever. Lord Varuna and Lord Vishnu intervened, forcing Ravana to place the lingam in Deoghar, where it stands eternally today.

Every monsoon during Shravan (July-August), millions of devotees clad in saffron robes undertake the arduous 105 km barefoot Kanwar Yatra from Sultanganj carrying sacred Ganga water to offer abhishekam to Baba Bholenath.

Top Sights to Combine on Your Deoghar Trip

  • Basukinath Dham (42 km): Known as the court of Lord Shiva (Faujdari Baba). Darshan at Baidyanath is traditionally completed only after paying respects at Basukinath.
  • Trikut Pahar (20 km): Three-peaked hill featuring India’s premier passenger ropeway/cable car with breathtaking panoramic vistas over Santhal Pargana.
  • Shivganga Kund: The sacred water tank in front of the temple complex where pilgrims take a holy dip before entering the sanctum.
  • Naulakha Mandir: A magnificent 146-foot high temple dedicated to Radha-Krishna, built in 1948 by Queen Charushila of Pathuria.

What are the temple darshan timings at Baidyanath Dham?

The temple opens at 04:00 AM for VIP and early morning Kacha Jal offerings, closes between 03:30 PM to 06:00 PM for Bhog, and reopens from 06:00 PM until Shringar Aarti at 09:00 PM.

Can elderly pilgrims get VIP Darshan passes?

Yes. Quick Darshan (Sheeghra Darshanam) coupons priced at ₹500 per devotee are available at temple administrative counters to bypass general queue waiting.
