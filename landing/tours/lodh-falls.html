About Lodh Falls — Nature's Untamed Wonder

Tucked away in the pristine wilderness of Mahuadanr valley in Latehar district, Lodh Falls (also revered as Budha Ghagh Falls) is the undisputed king of Jharkhand's waterfalls. Plummeting down multiple rocky tiers from an astounding height of 143 meters (469 feet), the sound of the cascading Burha River echoes through the dense Sal and Mahua forests for over 10 kilometers.

Popular 2-Day Netarhat + Lodh Falls Combo Itinerary

Most travelers choose to combine Netarhat Queen of Chotanagpur with Lodh Falls:

  • Day 1: Pickup from Ranchi at 07:00 AM, drive through Kudunghat to Netarhat. Check into hotel, explore Koel View Point and Magnoliya Sunset Point.
  • Day 2: Early morning Sunrise Point view, breakfast, followed by a scenic 60 km drive down to Lodh Falls. Relax by the waterfall, lunch at Mahuadanr, and return smoothly to Ranchi by 07:30 PM.

Traveler Tips

  • Vehicle Choice: An SUV or comfortable Sedan is highly recommended for the winding hilly roads.
  • Fuel & Refreshments: Carry snacks and refill essentials at Mahuadanr town, as facilities right at the waterfall gate are limited.

Is it safe to visit Lodh Falls with family?

Yes, tourism infrastructure has improved with proper asphalt roads, local tourism eco-guides, and District Car's GPS-tracked verified vehicles ensuring 100% peace of mind.

Can we swim in the pool below Lodh Falls?

Dipping feet in shallow rocky edges is fine, but swimming directly below the deep waterfall plunge pool is prohibited due to powerful undercurrents.
