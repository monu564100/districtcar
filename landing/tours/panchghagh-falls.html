About Panchghagh Falls — The Peaceful Oasis of Khunti

Panchghagh is formed by five distinct streams of the Banai River flowing gently across wide, polished granite stone shelves. Because the streams are shallow, tranquil, and free of dangerous undercurrents, it is recognized as Jharkhand’s safest waterfall for families with small children and senior citizens.
