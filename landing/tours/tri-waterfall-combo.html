The Ultimate 1-Day Waterfalls Road Trip from Ranchi

Visiting Ranchi without witnessing its world-class waterfalls is incomplete. But booking separate cabs for each spot is costly and exhausting. The Tri-Waterfall Circuit Combo by District Car solves this by packaging the three greatest waterfalls of Jharkhand into one stress-free, all-inclusive private tour.

You get picked up directly from your doorstep in Ranchi at 07:30 AM in a clean, sanitized AC vehicle. Your dedicated chauffeur navigates the winding forest roads between Ormanjhi, Angara, and Bundu, waits comfortably at each scenic spot, and returns you safely before evening.

Detailed Hour-by-Hour Circuit Schedule

  • 07:30 AM: Doorstep pickup from your hotel, railway station, or home in Ranchi.
  • 08:45 AM – 11:15 AM: Spot 1: Hundru Falls (322 ft). Enjoy the majestic Subarnarekha drop, walk down the stone stairway, and sip piping hot village chai.
  • 12:00 PM – 02:00 PM: Spot 2: Jonha Falls (Gautamdhara). Visit the hanging valley waterfall and ancient Lord Buddha Ashram. Enjoy a traditional lunch at local dhabas.
  • 03:15 PM – 05:00 PM: Spot 3: Dassam Falls. Experience the 10 gushing streams of Kanchi River as the afternoon sun casts rainbows through the water mist.
  • 06:30 PM: Comfortable drop-off back at your Ranchi location.
