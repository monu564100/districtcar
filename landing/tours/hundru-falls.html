About Hundru Falls — The Crown of Chotanagpur Plateau

Hundru Falls is ranked the 34th highest waterfall in India and is arguably the most famous waterfall in Jharkhand. As the sacred Subarnarekha River plunges over a massive vertical rock precipice from a height of 98 meters (322 feet), it creates a thunderous roar and mist clouds that shroud the surrounding Sal forest.

At the base of the waterfall, the swirling waters carve natural circular bathing pools and polished stone gorges over millions of years. It is a premier picnic and excursion hub for families, couples, and nature enthusiasts from Ranchi, Purulia, Bokaro, and Jamshedpur.

Safety & Family Tips

  • Guard Railings: The water current near the deep plunge pool has strong whirlpools. Always heed tourism safety signs and stay behind protective railings.
  • Footwear: Wear comfortable sports shoes with rubber soles; wet stone steps can be slippery.
  • Accessibility: Descending 350 steps is easy, but ascending takes moderate effort. Shaded benches are placed every 50 steps for elders to catch their breath.
  • Mobile Network: Jio and Airtel have 4G/5G connectivity throughout the viewpoint and parking area.
