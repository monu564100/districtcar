About Patratu Valley — The Grand Ghat Road of Jharkhand

Located just 38 km north-west of Ranchi in Ramgarh district, Patratu Valley has rapidly evolved into eastern India's most Instagrammable road trip destination. The highway carves down the hill through a mesmerizing series of green serpent-like S-curve hairpin loops offering commanding vantage points over the sprawling Patratu reservoir below.

At the base of the ghat lies the scenic Patratu Lake Resort. Constructed by Jharkhand Tourism, the complex offers modern amenities, speed-boating rides to small green islands, pedal boating, a water sports marina, and scenic walking promenades that glow brightly as the sun dips below the Chotanagpur hills.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is Patratu Valley safe for night travel?

The main ghat road is well paved and frequently patrolled, but for outstation tourists, daytime and sunset visits (returning before 8:30 PM) are strongly recommended. Our drivers are thoroughly verified local residents who navigate the curves safely.

How much does speed boating cost at Patratu Dam?

Motorboat rides start at ₹100 per passenger, while private high-speed speedboats are ₹400 to ₹600 for a thrilling 15-minute island round.
