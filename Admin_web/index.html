District Car Platform Rules
10% Commission Model

Drivers keep 90% of every ride. 10% platform maintenance fee is automatically logged to Driver Commission Wallet.

3-Trip Cash Lock Rule

When a driver accumulates 3 unpaid cash rides without clearing platform dues, driver app auto-locks new bookings until settled.

Instant UPI Settlement

Driver online trip payouts can be released via direct UPI reference numbers in the Payouts module.
