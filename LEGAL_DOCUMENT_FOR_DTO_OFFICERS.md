## 1. LEGAL STATUS AS AN "INTERMEDIARY" (IT ACT, 2000)

1.1. **Statutory Definition**: Under **Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000**, an *"Intermediary"* is defined as:
> *“Any person who on behalf of another person receives, stores or transmits that record or provides any service with respect to that record and includes telecom service providers, network service providers, internet service providers, web-hosting service providers, search engines, online payment sites, online-auction sites, online-market places and cyber cafes.”*

1.2. **No Fleet Ownership**: District Car is a **software platform (cloud software and mobile technology)** that enables independent, third-party vehicle operators and intending passengers to discover and communicate with each other. 
* District Car **owns zero motor vehicles**.
* District Car **employs zero drivers** (drivers are independent third-party operators).
* District Car is **not a public transport carrier, fleet owner, or stage carriage operator**.

1.3. **Statutory Safe Harbor (Section 79, IT Act, 2000)**: Under **Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000**, an intermediary is legally exempted from liability for third-party information, communications, and transport agreements entered into directly between the driver and the passenger:
> *“An intermediary shall not be liable for any third party information, data, or communication link made available or hosted by him...”*

## 2. COMPLIANCE WITH MOTOR VEHICLES ACT, 1988

2.1. **Strict Commercial Yellow-Plate Mandate (Section 66 Compliance)**:
* Under **Section 66 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988**, no private motor vehicle (white plate) may be used for carrying passengers for hire or reward.
* **District Car strictly enforces this statutory mandate.** The platform **prohibits private white-plate personal vehicles**.
* Every onboarded vehicle is pre-verified to possess:
  1. A valid **Commercial Yellow Plate Registration Certificate (RC)** issued by the State Transport Department.
  2. A valid **Commercial / Transport Driving License** with appropriate transport endorsement.
  3. A valid **Commercial Vehicle Insurance Policy** with third-party and passenger liability coverage.
  4. A valid **Fitness Certificate (FC)** and **Pollution Under Control (PUC)** certificate issued by authorized RTO testing stations.
  5. An authorized **Contract Carriage or Tourist Taxi Permit**.

2.2. **Independent Operator Responsibility**: Because the onboarded drivers already hold statutory commercial permits from the Transport Department under Section 66 of the Motor Vehicles Act, the platform does not issue transport permits nor does it operate as an unauthorized passenger carrier. It merely acts as a digital directory and dispatch communication tool.

## 3. ZERO FINANCIAL INTERMEDIATION & PAYMENT GATEWAY EXEMPTION

3.1. **Direct Peer-to-Peer Fare Settlement**:
* Riders pay **100% of the trip fare directly to the driver partner** upon completion of the trip in **Cash** or via the driver's **personal UPI QR code**.
* **District Car does NOT collect, hold, route, or settle passenger fares.**
* There is no pooled escrow account, no wallet holding passenger funds, and no third-party payment gateway merchant routing.

3.2. **Non-Applicability of RBI Payment Aggregator (PA/PG) Guidelines**:
* Under the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Guidelines on Regulation of Payment Aggregators and Payment Gateways (DPSS.CO.PD.No.1810/02.14.008/2019-20)**, regulatory licensing and nodal/escrow account requirements apply strictly to entities that *“handle, pool, and intermediate funds received from customers for onward settlement to merchants.”*
* Because District Car never takes custody of passenger trip money, it is **statutorily exempt** from RBI Payment Aggregator licensing and escrow banking compliance.

## 4. NON-MANDATORY STATUS OF CURRENT ACCOUNT & GST (BELOW ₹20 LAKH TURNOVER)

4.1. **Statutory Exemption under Goods & Services Tax (GST)**:
* Under **Section 22(1) of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017**:
  > *“Every supplier shall be liable to be registered under this Act in the State or Union territory... from where he makes a taxable supply of goods or services or both, if his aggregate turnover in a financial year exceeds twenty lakh rupees.”*
* The State of Jharkhand follows the statutory service threshold of **₹20,00,000 (Twenty Lakh Rupees)**.
* During the pilot, launch, and early operating stages, the platform's aggregate commission revenue from software platform fees is substantially below the ₹20 Lakh statutory threshold. Therefore, **GST registration is legally optional and not a prerequisite to start service**.

4.2. **Legality of Bank Account Usage**:
* In India, a Sole Proprietorship is legally synonymous with the proprietor/developer in their individual capacity.
* An individual testing a technology pilot under the micro-enterprise framework (Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Act, 2006) is legally permitted to receive platform technology service receipts without requiring an immediate corporate multi-branch commercial current account until transaction volumes reach formal statutory thresholds.

## 5. PUBLIC SAFETY, DISPATCH VERIFICATION & POLICE COOPERATION

District Car places passenger safety and local governance at the center of its technical architecture:

| Safety & Compliance Mechanism | Technical Architecture in District Car |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Driver Identity Verification** | Every driver partner is verified via Government Aadhaar, Commercial DL, and Police Character Verification (*Acharan Praman Patra*). |
| **Trip Start Authentication (OTP)** | A secure 4-digit One-Time Password (OTP) generated on the customer's phone must be entered into the driver's app before a trip can physically start, preventing ghost trips or unauthorized vehicle substitutions. |
| **Live Route & GPS Telemetry** | Every active ride is digitally logged with origin, destination, timestamp, and route telemetry. |
| **Law Enforcement Protocol** | In compliance with Rule 3 of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines) Rules, 2021, District Car maintains full capability to assist law enforcement agencies within 72 hours of receiving formal notice. |
| **Emergency SOS Functionality** | Direct access to local police emergency helpline (112) integrated within the customer mobile app. |
