## 1. LEGAL STATUS AS AN "INTERMEDIARY" (IT ACT, 2000)

1.1. **Statutory Definition**: Under **Section 2(1)(w) of the Information Technology Act, 2000**, an *"Intermediary"* is defined as:
> *“Any person who on behalf of another person receives, stores or transmits that record or provides any service with respect to that record and includes telecom service providers, network service providers, internet service providers, web-hosting service providers, search engines, online payment sites, online-auction sites, online-market places and cyber cafes.”*

1.2. **No Physical Presence in Rides**: District Car is a **cloud software communication tool and mobile application**. 
* The platform administrators and software developers **never physically meet** the vehicle owner or the co-passenger.
* District Car **owns zero motor vehicles**.
* District Car **employs zero drivers** (all drivers/vehicle owners are independent private citizens or operators).
* District Car is **not a public transport carrier, fleet owner, or stage carriage operator**.

1.3. **Statutory Safe Harbor (Section 79, IT Act, 2000)**: Under **Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000**, an electronic intermediary is legally exempted from liability for third-party communications, agreements, and interactions entered into directly between users of the software:
> *“An intermediary shall not be liable for any third party information, data, or communication link made available or hosted by him...”*

## 3. PRIVATE WHITE-PLATE COMMUNITY CARPOOLING & COST-SHARING FRAMEWORK

3.1. **Legal Precedent for Private Vehicle Ridesharing (The BlaBlaCar / Quick Ride Model)**:
* Under the **Motor Vehicles Act, 1988**, a private personal vehicle (white plate) cannot be operated as a commercial taxi for commercial *"hire or reward"*.
* **HOWEVER, genuine non-commercial carpooling and travel expense-sharing is legally distinct from commercial taxi operations.**
* When private citizens travelling in the same direction connect with family, friends, neighbors, or co-commuters to share travel expenses (fuel, tolls, and routine running expenses):
  1. The vehicle owner is **already undertaking the journey** for their own personal, family, medical, or employment purpose.
  2. The financial contribution from co-commuters is strictly a **cost-sharing mechanism** (reimbursement of actual fuel and highway costs) and **does not constitute commercial profit, commercial hire, or taxi fare**.
  3. No commercial stage-carriage or taxi tariff is charged.

3.2. **Self-Drive & Family Networking Principle**:
* District Car functions as an electronic bulletin board connecting district residents, families, and co-commuters travelling between villages, blocks, and district centers where public transit frequency is low.
* Private vehicle owners list their upcoming travel itineraries; fellow community members simply request to occupy vacant seats on an expense-sharing basis.

## 4. STRICT ABSOLUTE LIABILITY & COMPLETE INDEMNITY CLAUSE (ON DRIVERS)

4.1. **100% Driver / Vehicle Owner Liability**:
* Every private vehicle owner who registers on District Car is bound by an enforceable **Electronic Terms of Service & User Indemnity Agreement**.
* The platform explicitly requires the vehicle owner to warrant that:
  1. They hold a valid, non-expired Driving License issued under the Motor Vehicles Act.
  2. The vehicle possesses a valid Private Registration Certificate (RC), valid Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate, and active Motor Insurance (covering Third-Party Liability as mandated by Section 146 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988).
  3. The vehicle is in a safe, roadworthy, and mechanically sound condition.
  4. The vehicle owner drives with due care and abides by all traffic laws and speed regulations.

4.2. **Statutory Disclaimer of Mishap & Accident Liability**:
> **NON-LIABILITY DECLARATION**:  
> *District Car provides only the electronic matching software. It exercises no physical custody, mechanical inspection, or direct supervision over the vehicle, driver, or journey. In the event of any road accident, mishap, personal injury, property damage, traffic challan, dispute, or criminal act occurring during or in connection with the journey, the sole and exclusive legal, civil, and penal liability rests upon the driver and owner of the motor vehicle. District Car, its creators, administrators, and hosting providers are completely indemnified and hold zero liability under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000.*

## 5. ZERO FINANCIAL INTERMEDIATION & PAYMENT GATEWAY EXEMPTION

5.1. **Direct Peer-to-Peer Settlement**:
* Co-travellers settle their shared fuel and travel expenses **directly with the vehicle owner** in **Cash** or via the driver's **personal UPI QR code**.
* **District Car does NOT collect, hold, route, or escrow any ride money.**
* No funds pass through the platform's accounts for ride transactions.

5.2. **Statutory Exemption from RBI Payment Aggregator (PA/PG) Mandates**:
* Under the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Guidelines on Regulation of Payment Aggregators and Payment Gateways (DPSS.CO.PD.No.1810/02.14.008/2019-20)**, regulatory licensing and escrow banking apply only to entities that *“handle, pool, and intermediate funds received from customers for onward settlement to merchants.”*
* Because District Car never takes custody of passenger money, it is **statutorily exempt** from RBI Payment Aggregator licensing and nodal/escrow account requirements.

## 6. NON-MANDATORY STATUS OF CURRENT ACCOUNT & GST (BELOW ₹20 LAKH TURNOVER)

6.1. **Statutory GST Exemption Threshold**:
* Under **Section 22(1) of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017**:
  > *“Every supplier shall be liable to be registered under this Act in the State or Union territory... from where he makes a taxable supply of goods or services or both, if his aggregate turnover in a financial year exceeds twenty lakh rupees.”*
* In Jharkhand, service providers with aggregate annual turnover below **₹20,00,000 (Twenty Lakh Rupees)** are **statutorily exempted from GST registration**.
* During this technology pilot, the platform's nominal software maintenance fees are well below ₹20 Lakhs, making GST registration completely optional.

6.2. **Legality of Bank Account Usage**:
* In India, an individual entrepreneur or sole developer running an early-stage software pilot under the Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development (MSMED) Act, 2006 is legally permitted to operate without an immediate corporate multi-branch commercial current account until transaction volumes reach formal statutory thresholds.
